9. Trend Monetization Multi-Angle Framework Prompt: 

Take this trending topic: [Insert Trend/Topic Here]. 
Generate a 4-post sequence that maximizes engagement. 
1. The Spark: start it with (breaking, just in): Fast, attention-grabbing post 
2. Curiosity Gap: Highlight a detail people want to know but leave it incomplete. Encourage guesses. 
3. Controversy: Introduce a polarizing perspective or comparison. Use numbers, status, to fuel debate. 
4. Speculation: Predict outcomes or future events. Include unexpected or controversial elements. prompt to keep conversation alive. 
Posts should be short, punchy, visual-friendly, and fit the platform’s tone. no sentences with question marks


10. Paraphrasing Prompt for Any Text:
 
Paraphrase This Text: 
[Insert text here] 
Instructions: 
1. Read the text carefully and understand the main ideas. 
2. Rewrite it completely in your own words using one of these methods: 
• Tell-a-Friend: Explain it as if telling a friend. 
• Chunking: Break it into key phrases, rewrite each, then combine. 
3. Use different grammar and vocabulary; avoid copying more than 4–5 words in a row. 
4. Keep all main points, do not add new ideas. 
5. Include the source or author, e.g., (Author, Year). 
6. Make sure your version is clear, readable, and matches the meaning of the original. 
Output: One fully paraphrased version of the text.

Midjourney Image prompts for any subject (Note: you can use these prompts on any image generator out there in the market right now.)

1. Close-up Shot: Focus tight on the subject. Highlight texture, color, and fine detail. Works well for faces, objects, products, animals, or scenery.
 
Prompt:
 
Close-up of [subject], realistic, soft lighting, rich texture, high detail, sharp focus --v 6 --ar 4:5 

2. Mid-angle Shot: Frame the subject from a moderate distance. Enough context to understand the environment without being far away. 

Prompt:
 
Mid-angle shot of [subject], [location or environment], natural light, photorealistic --v 6 

3. Candid Shot: Show the subject in action. Treat it like a moment that was not staged, with human movement or natural motion. 

Prompt:
 
Candid scene of [subject], natural light, lifestyle setting, storytelling composition --v 6

4. Flat Lay or Top-down Shot: Top-down view. Useful for collections, desk setups, outfits, gear, art supplies, or any arrangement. 

Prompt:
 
Top-down shot of [subject], realistic, vibrant, clean composition, taken with an iPhone 17 Pro, casual photo style --v 6 --ar 1:1
