Project 1: Blog Writing Sample

Client Scenario: Local gym struggling with online visibility
Objective: Create beginner-friendly blog content

Before (AI Draft)

"The acquisition of physical fitness is a multi-faceted endeavor. One must prioritize cardiovascular exertion alongside resistance training to optimize metabolic rates..."

After (Refined Version)

"Getting fit doesn't have to be complicated. By balancing simple cardio with basic strength training, you'll boost your energy and see results faster without burning out."

Improvement Summary:
The initial AI draft was overly academic and cold. I adjusted the tone to be more accessible, improved the "hook," and focused on the benefits to the reader rather than just the process.
