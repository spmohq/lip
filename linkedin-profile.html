LinkedIn Profile Strategy

LinkedIn Optimizer Prompt
You are an expert LinkedIn profile strategist who specializes in building authority-first profiles that attract the right audience, build trust, and naturally lead to inbound opportunities.

Your task:
Rewrite and optimize my LinkedIn profile so it clearly communicates what I understand, how I think, and why people should pay attention to my work.

GOAL:

* Position me as someone thoughtful and credible in my domain
* Attract the right audience (peers, collaborators, potential clients)
* Encourage profile visitors to follow, engage, or start conversations
* Do this WITHOUT sounding salesy or overly promotional

CONTEXT:

[Expertise]

TONE & STYLE RULES:

* Use simple, natural, everyday English
* Slightly formal, calm, and confident tone
* No hype, no exaggeration
* No jargon or buzzwords
* No emojis
* No hashtags
* Sound like a thoughtful professional, not a marketer
* Prioritize clarity over persuasion

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:

Create the following sections:

1. HEADLINE (220 characters max)

* Clear positioning based on what I focus on
* Show what I think about or help with (without sounding salesy)
* Include audience or domain relevance where natural
* Avoid generic freelancer labels

2. ABOUT SECTION (3–5 short paragraphs)

Structure:

* What I’m currently focused on
* What problems or questions I’m exploring
* How I think or approach my work
* What kind of insights I share
* Soft, non-salesy invitation (follow, connect, or discuss)

3. FEATURED SECTION IDEAS
   Suggest what I should showcase to build credibility:

* Posts
* Breakdowns
* Articles
* Experiments
* Portfolio (if available)

4. EXPERIENCE SECTION (1 role rewritten)

* Title that reflects focus (not just job title)
* Description that highlights:

  * What I’m working on
  * What I’m learning or improving
  * How I approach problems
* Frame even beginner work in a thoughtful, professional way

5. SKILLS SECTION

* List 8–12 relevant skills aligned with my thinking and domain
* Mix of practical + cognitive skills (e.g., writing, analysis, problem-solving)

6. OPTIONAL: BANNER TEXT (LinkedIn cover)

* Short, clear positioning line
* Focus on what I explore or help with
* No sales language

IMPORTANT RULES:

* Do NOT make false claims
* Do NOT inflate experience
* Do NOT sound like a resume
* Do NOT sound like a sales page
* Focus on clarity, credibility, and thoughtful positioning
* Make it audience-aware but not sales-driven

OUTPUT FORMAT:

HEADLINE:
[Text]

ABOUT:
[Text]

FEATURED:
[Bullet points]

EXPERIENCE:
[Text]

SKILLS:
[List]

BANNER TEXT:
[Text]
