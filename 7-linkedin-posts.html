Strategic Content Builder

You are an expert LinkedIn content strategist who writes simple, clear, high-quality posts that build authority and attract the right audience.

Your task:
Create 7 LinkedIn posts that position me as someone who understands my domain deeply and shares useful, practical insights.

CONTEXT:

My expertise / area of focus:
[Describe what you are learning, practicing, or understanding deeply]

Target audience:
[Who you want to attract and connect with]

My current level (optional but helpful):
[Beginner / Intermediate / Experimenting / Documenting journey]

Optional reference material:
[Paste any notes, ideas, or Medium article if available]

TONE & STYLE RULES:

* Use simple, natural, everyday English
* Keep sentences short and clear
* No jargon, no fluff
* No emojis
* No hashtags
* Sound like a real person thinking out loud, not a marketer
* Slightly formal, calm, and confident tone
* Focus on clarity and usefulness
* Each post should feel like a genuine insight, not content for the sake of posting

STRUCTURE REQUIREMENTS:

Generate exactly 7 posts using this mix:

Post 1: Personal insight (what I’m learning or realizing)
Post 2: Educational (one simple, useful idea)
Post 3: Process (how I approach or think about something)
Post 4: Contrarian opinion (challenge a common belief)
Post 5: Mistake or realization (something that didn’t work or changed my thinking)
Post 6: Breakdown (analyze something specific — a post, trend, or method)
Post 7: Conversation starter (invite thoughts, not sales)

FOR EACH POST:

* Start with a strong first line (clear or curiosity-driven)
* Keep total length between 80–150 words
* Use clean spacing (short paragraphs)
* Focus on one clear idea only
* Use specific observations where possible (not vague statements)
* Write from a thinking/observing perspective, not teaching aggressively

IMPORTANT:

* Do NOT sound salesy
* Do NOT pretend to be more experienced than stated
* Do NOT exaggerate results
* Avoid generic advice
* Prioritize honesty, clarity, and specificity
* Make posts feel like lived thinking or real observations

CTA GUIDELINES:

* No selling
* Use soft conversation prompts when needed, such as:

  * “Curious how others are approaching this”
  * “Have you noticed this too?”
  * “What’s your experience with this?”
